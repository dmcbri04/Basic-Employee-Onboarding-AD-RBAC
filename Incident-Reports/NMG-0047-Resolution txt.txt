Ticket number: NMG-0047

Affected user: Jane Cooper (jcooper@NMG.com)

Issue reported:
Jane reported that she could not access HR shared resources and that her desktop policies and mapped drives differed from those of her HR teammates.

Root cause:
During the investigation, I identified two account configuration issues. Jane’s account was located in the Operations Users organizational unit (OU) instead of the HR OU. Her account also belonged to the Operations-Users group and was missing the required HR-Users group membership. These settings did not match her role as an HR Payroll Specialist.

Fix applied:
Moved Jane’s account from the Operations Users OU to the HR OU. Removed her account from the incorrect Operations-Users group and added it to the HR-Users group to align her account configuration with her department and job responsibilities.

Verification:
Confirmed in Active Directory Users and Computers that Jane’s account (jcooper) appeared in the HR OU. Reviewed the account’s Member Of tab and confirmed that HR-Users was listed and Operations-Users was no longer listed. Also opened the HR-Users group and confirmed that Jane appeared in its member list. Captured screenshots documenting the corrected OU placement and group membership.
